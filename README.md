## Featured work

**[Iraq Service Atlas](https://github.com/murtadha203/iraq-service-atlas-dashboard)**\
135,000+ documented service points across Iraq, classified into 16 categories and analysed on 4,582 national 10 km cells and 435,000+ cells at 1 km, instead of governorate averages that hide the pattern. Interactive Arabic dashboard and an Arabic analytical report.\
[Dashboard](https://murtadha203.github.io/iraq-service-atlas-dashboard/)\
`Python` `GeoPandas` `MapLibre GL` `PMTiles`

**[Iraqi Job Market Pipeline](https://github.com/murtadha203/iraq-etl-pipeline)**\
101,961 job posts over 17 months, from 30+ Telegram channels and 10+ hiring platforms. Deduplicated, screened with a multi-stage AI classifier, and extracted into title, company, location, salary, experience, education and skills. The result is a structured dataset and an Arabic report on Iraq's private-sector job market.\
[Report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1AFzO-YXJrak-Fgvl5awe1navz9SDI7u7/view?usp=drivesdk)\
`Python` `Scrapy` `Playwright` `Telethon` `Arabic NLP`

**[Hierarchical LLM Control for 5G/6G](https://github.com/murtadha203/hierarchical-llm-5g-control)** (graduation project)\
Deterministic agents run the radio; an LLM picks the strategy, behind a safety shield that blocks unsafe output. In 600-second simulations across seeds: 73% fewer URLLC drops, 57% less mMTC energy, 70% fewer ping-pong handovers.\
`Python` `LangGraph` `Multi-agent systems`

**Arabic Maqam Classification**\
Recognising 8 Arabic maqamat in music and in Quran recitation from pitch features with a CNN-LSTM model. 92.72% test accuracy.\
[Music notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/murtadhanajim/arabic-maqam-classification-in-music) | [Quran notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/murtadhanajim/quran-maqam-classification)\
`Librosa` `TensorFlow` `Keras`

**Digital Modulation Classification**\
A family classifier routes each I/Q signal to a specialised CNN for ASK, FSK, PSK, APSK or QAM. 97.2% overall accuracy.\
[Notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/murtadhanajim/modulation-classification)\
`TensorFlow` `Signal processing`
